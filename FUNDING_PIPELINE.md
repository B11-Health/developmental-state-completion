## Immediate 45-day proposal strategy

### Track 1 - NSF Mathematical Biology
Working title:
**MB: Counterfactual State Completion: Mathematics for Identifying Developmental Mechanisms from Minimal Perturbations**

Core intellectual question:
> What finite family of interventions and measurements is sufficient to distinguish future-relevant developmental state-law equivalence classes, and how stable is that distinction under finite resolution and noise?

Required strengthening before submission:
- formal theorem/counterexample package from Math Lane;
- primary-literature novelty audit;
- at least one public biological dataset demonstration;
- credible biological collaborator or advisory structure;
- clear separation of established Reeb/observability mathematics from the proposed new contribution.

### Track 2 - NSF BIO Core / Developmental Systems
Working title:
**Prospective Developmental State Completion in Plants Using Minimal Counterfactual Perturbations**

Core biological question:
> Can two biological backgrounds that are difficult to distinguish at baseline be prospectively separated by a frozen, small perturbation panel selected to expose future-relevant developmental state?

Required strengthening before submission:
- actual plant system + genotypes/reporters;
- collaborator able to execute the perturbations;
- preregistered blinded protocol;
- power/sample-size rationale;
- physiological perturbation-cost definition, not silhouette IoU;
- baseline morphology and random/equal-cost perturbation comparators.
