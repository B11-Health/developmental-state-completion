# Outreach Pack

## Scientific community announcement

We have opened a public checkpoint of Code Gym's developmental-state research:

https://github.com/B11-Health/developmental-state-completion

The central question is simple: **what must we know about a living system right now so that its older measured history becomes unnecessary for predicting what it can do next?**

Our current computational program combines predictive screening-off, counterfactual perturbation design, and topology of connected inverse phenotype fibers. The repo includes both positive and negative results, including a calibration failure showing why finite-estimator history gain cannot by itself prove Markov state closure.

The strongest candidate idea is **developmental state completion**: if history still improves prediction after conditioning on the present state, treat that as evidence that the state representation is incomplete, then choose the next measurement or perturbation to expose the missing future-relevant direction.

This is preprint-stage work, not peer reviewed, and several numerical results still need code-level migration and independent reproduction. We are publishing now specifically to invite criticism, replication, and collaboration before making stronger claims.

We are particularly interested in collaborators in plant development, lineage-resolved imaging, causal inference, dynamical systems, active experimental design, and topological data analysis.

## Collaboration email core

Subject: Open computational research on developmental state completion

We have opened a public checkpoint of a computational developmental-biology program focused on a specific question: what present-state measurements are sufficient to make older measured history redundant for predicting future responses across a defined perturbation family?

The current work combines calibrated predictive screening-off with counterfactual experimental design and connected inverse-fiber topology. We are deliberately not claiming a biological law at this stage; the repository separates reanalysis, simulation, calibration failures, hypotheses, and the prospective experiment that would falsify the framework.

Repository: https://github.com/B11-Health/developmental-state-completion

We would value critical feedback, especially on the novelty boundary, the state-sufficiency formulation, and the design of a blinded living-system validation.
