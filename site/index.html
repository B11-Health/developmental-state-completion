Preprint-stage computational research · not peer reviewed

What must we know about a living system right now so that its older measured history becomes unnecessary for predicting what it can do next?

Developmental state completion is an experimental program for testing that question without assuming that morphology, transcriptomics, lineage, or any single measurement is “the state.” A candidate state is task-relative: it must predict a specified future, and older measured history should add little information once that current state is known.

Evidence discipline

Different claims carry different evidence.

The repository’s claim ledger keeps observation, simulation, calibration, interpretation, and hypothesis separate. This page also marks whether an artifact is reproducible, historical, or still prospective.

01

Observed-data reanalysis

Results reconstructed from previously published biological datasets. They can support task-specific empirical patterns, not universal biological laws.

02

Synthetic-model result

Claims established inside a declared simulator or constructed hidden-world system. They test logic and algorithms, not living biology.

03

Calibration result

Known-complete and known-history-dependent controls used to quantify estimator bias, false positives, and detection power.

04

Interpretation

An explanatory synthesis consistent with current results. Interpretation is deliberately not labeled as direct measurement.

05

Hypothesis

A prospective claim that requires a new experiment. The living-plant state-completion test remains in this class.

Strongest observed-data result

Late-L1 Arabidopsis FM1: current atlas state predicts future growth; older released history adds no material gain under the tested decoders.

In the predefined 96→120→132 h L1 cohort (256 cells / 86 ancestor groups), lineage-grouped analyses show a strong contribution from the current 25-channel atlas state beyond current geometry. In the direct-source checkpoint, nested Ridge rises from R² 0.3634 to 0.5947, then to 0.5956 after adding 96 h ancestor history. The larger repeated-split audit reports median Ridge history gain −0.015 R², positive in only 4% of partitions and never above +0.05.

Critical boundary: the 25 channels are integrated binary atlas annotations assembled from prior expression information, not simultaneous longitudinal molecular measurements in the same living cells. This is task-, stage-, representation-, decoder-, and finite-sample-relative predictive screening-off—not proof of conditional independence or plant-wide Markovity.

Cross-system C. elegans replication

Older lineage timing produced large nonlinear held-out gains beyond a coarse present state, but the preregistered stable-history decision was not promoted because calibration power was too low.

In 701 eligible dividing tracks from two developing C. elegans embryos, group-aware repeated splits compared a coarse present state S (birth time, lineage depth, embryo identity) with S plus parent/grandparent timing history H. Median history gain was +0.171 R² for HistGradientBoosting and +0.134 R² for random forest, positive on 96% and 93% of primary grouped splits.

Those observed medians exceeded matched known-complete null cutoffs, but power for the preregistered +0.30 target-SD known-incomplete control was only 17.5%, 21.0%, and 39.0% across the three estimators. The rule required at least 80% power for two estimators, so the stable-residual-history criterion was not met.

Boundary: this is a calibration-limited result, not a null result and not evidence that true C. elegans biology is non-Markov. S is intentionally coarse; no molecular-memory or organism-wide claim follows.

Independently reproducible 128-world simulator result

A finite 128-world library can be separated by two contexts at exact resolution—and the frozen two-context source test passed every preregistered prediction.

The preserved source-derived library contains 128 candidate worlds, 16 available contexts, and 34 phenotype coordinates per context. Exhaustive finite separation finds a minimum exact panel size of 2, with 40 sufficient two-context panels; all use contexts at Hamming distance 3 or 4.

The separate prospective source-render test froze predictions before rendering: across 32 complementary pairs, sign recovery was 100% and maximum signed-state L2 error was 0.000514625. A separately frozen 64-context extension then achieved 100% sign recovery for all five theoretically sufficient Hamming≥3 masks. These are source-simulator results, not living-plant evidence.

T7 robust topological completion

A one-bit branch ambiguity can impose a sharp worst-case prediction floor when continuous measurement dimension is too small.

For the classical antipodal quotient h:S^n → RP^n with future target F(x)=x, Borsuk–Ulam forces an antipodal completed-measurement collision for every continuous augmentation B:S^n→R^k with k≤n. At that collision any decoder receives the same input for futures distance 2 apart, so its worst-case Euclidean error is at least 1. The constant-zero decoder attains exactly 1, making the minimax value sharp.

For every tolerance epsilon<1, n+1 continuous Euclidean channels are necessary and sufficient in this example; B(x)=x gives zero error and antipodal separation margin 2.

Boundary: this is established topology plus an elementary decision-theoretic corollary, not a new Borsuk–Ulam theorem and not evidence that a biological system has this topology. The independent T7A audit passed the central results after explicit Hausdorff/codomain/noise-scope qualifications.

R8 morphology + intensity rescue

Adding release-native morphology proxies and raw intensity still did not make the frozen future tasks transfer across both acquisitions.

R8 expanded the present-only representation from 49 to 139 features in Drosophila and from 46 to 99 features in Tribolium using only frames 23–25: relational/kinematic state, GT/TRA tracking-mask geometry proxies, shape changes, local context, and raw-image intensity/gradient/polarity summaries.

The preregistered gate required at least two of Ridge, Random Forest, and Extra Trees to achieve positive held-out R² and beat the train-only mean baseline in both reciprocal acquisitions. All four organism×outcome tasks failed. Tribolium radial prediction reached about +0.30 R² for RF/Extra Trees in sequence 02 but remained negative in sequence 01, so the favorable fold was not promoted.

Mandatory stop: because absolute adequacy failed, R8 did not fit older history and did not run history permutation/calibration. GT/TRA masks are geometry proxies, not segmentation ground truth; raw intensity is not a molecular assay. This is a transfer/representation limitation, not evidence of biological memory.

M2 exact perturbation optimization

Greedy matched the exact optimum on every audited 128-world row, while the same objective remains non-submodular and unsafe in general.

M2 exhaustively audited 128 truths × 9 tolerances × 3 declared topologies × 5 budgets = 17,280 rows. Across complete-survivor, law-MST × Q4, and law-clique × Q4 topologies, the lexicographic greedy panel had 0 suboptimal rows, minimum greedy/optimal ratio 1.0, and maximum additive gap 0.

That is an instance result, not a theorem. Concrete diminishing-returns violations remain in the source topology, and the frozen complementary-wall grid still gives f(greedy)/f(optimal)=3/n → 0. M2 therefore rejects any topology-free constant-factor guarantee inferred from the bundle.

New safe structural condition: if every biconnected block contains at most one vertex that any admissible experiment can directly delete, truth-rooted disconnection reduces to fixed-set coverage. Under that block-sparse deletion condition, the objective is monotone submodular and classical cardinality-greedy inherits the usual coverage guarantee. This is a sufficient condition, not a claim about arbitrary cyclic graphs.

LARRY / Weinreb higher-dimensional incompleteness control

The separated-sister signal survives conditioning on direct release-native day-2 expression panels up to 32 genes.

R3 replaces the earlier 2D-only present-state proxy with direct day-2 expression panels of 4, 8, 16, and 32 frozen genes plus starting population. Across both balanced multinomial logistic regression and histogram gradient boosting, adding the separated day-6 sister-well fate still improves held-out prediction of the other day-6 well. At expr32, the held-out log-loss gain is +0.328 for logistic regression and +0.523 for histogram gradient boosting.

Critical boundary: the sister-well label is a lineage-linked incompleteness diagnostic, not literal older history. The 32-gene panel is not the full transcriptome; SPRING itself was built from broader transcriptomic information. The checked-in null has only five permutations, so it is directional calibration rather than precision significance testing.

M3 robust cost-aware perturbation design

Prospective design now treats the truth as unknown and certifies tight-budget panels by exact finite optimization under declared stress and failure scenarios.

The preregistration policy forbids choosing a living-pilot panel with a known simulator truth or post-hoc biological outcome. Under the currently encoded synthetic cost, burden, failure-probability, and tolerance/noise proxies, the exact tight-budget planning panel is 0010 0100 1000 (three perturbations; synthetic cost 36 and burden 15).

The optimizer includes exact enumeration, subset-DP construction, a MILP cross-check for costed pair coverage, tolerance/noise stress, independent experiment failure, and minimax / one-dropout robustness. The robustification warning is explicit: pointwise minimax and worst-case one-failure dropout objectives are not generally submodular, even when a base coverage objective is.

Not a biological protocol recommendation: the current costs, burden units, failure probabilities, and noise/tolerance values are planning proxies. They must be replaced and frozen from a real protocol before a living run.

B2 living-system feasibility

The Arabidopsis interface can proceed to Phase 0 only conditionally; the confirmatory RCOg-V study is not ready to run today.

The preferred three-class concept remains RCOg-V, cuc2-4;RCOg-V, and one post-initiation suppressor, with functional PIN1:GFP and dex-inducible ChCUC1. B2 verified the published biological interfaces but did not verify a complete transferable combined-material path.

The current confirmatory decision is NO-GO because exact combined materials and transfer rights, GFP/VENUS/tdTomato optical compatibility, and acceptable perturbation burden remain unresolved. Phase 0 is a conditional GO only after collaborator/material-path confirmation, and it may change dose, stage, time, or feature choices before they are frozen.

No living-validation escalation: a Phase-0 feasibility GO is not evidence that state completion works in living plants. If only the Hu Col-0 inducible/PIN1 system is transferable, the allowed fallback is a method-transfer feasibility study, not an RCO hidden-genotype confirmatory test.

Prominent boundary

What we are NOT claiming.

These exclusions are part of the scientific result. They prevent a task-specific predictive pattern, finite simulator audit, or readiness decision from being promoted into a biological law or institutional fact.

  • We are not claiming that plants—or living systems generally—are Markov systems.
  • We are not claiming that the C. elegans preregistered stable-history criterion was met; calibration power was inadequate.
  • We are not claiming molecular memory or organism-wide non-Markovity from lineage timing.
  • We are not treating the LARRY separated-sister fate as literal history, or the tested 32-gene day-2 panel as a full-transcriptome state.
  • We are not treating the five-permutation R3 null as precision significance testing.
  • We are not claiming that morphology uniquely determines mechanism.
  • We are not claiming that one molecular principal component is a universal developmental state or an intrinsic one-dimensional biology.
  • We are not claiming that predictive screening-off, causal-state ideas, PSRs, Reeb-space topology, observability, or the rectifier algebra are mathematically new.
  • We are not inferring a general greedy guarantee from its exact match on the audited 128-world bundle; the objective remains non-submodular and can make greedy arbitrarily bad.
  • We are not claiming that minimax or one-failure dropout robustification preserves submodularity in general.
  • We are not treating the M3 tight-budget panel or its synthetic cost, burden, failure, and noise proxies as a living protocol recommendation.
  • We are not claiming that two perturbations are universally sufficient in living biology.
  • We are not treating the historical 224-world experiment or topology thresholds as currently reproducible.
  • We are not treating source-simulator validation—or a Phase-0 feasibility GO—as living-plant validation.
  • We are not claiming the confirmatory RCOg-V study is ready today.
  • We are not claiming Research.gov organization readiness, PI affiliation, AOR authority, or SPO/equivalent routing has been verified; the F3 browser session was unauthenticated and COI/F&A/disclosure gates remain unresolved.
  • We are not claiming that current results justify medical, agricultural, or clinical intervention.
