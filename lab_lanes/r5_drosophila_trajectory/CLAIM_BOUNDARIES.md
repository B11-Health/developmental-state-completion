# R5 Claim Boundaries

## Supported
- Public CTC gold tracking masks permit a two-acquisition Drosophila nervous-system cell trajectory stress test without downloading the full raw movie.
- 392 labeled cells were recovered at all six selected frames: 189 in sequence 01 and 203 in sequence 02.
- The frozen raw-vector task had poor cross-sequence predictive adequacy; all mean held-out R2 values were negative.
- A secondary radial-velocity representation produced consistently positive H increments at richer S across three estimators, but the augmented predictors remained worse than a train-only dummy baseline.
- R5 therefore motivates an absolute predictive-adequacy gate before interpreting residual-history gains.

## Not supported
- Older kinematic history is a stable biological memory signal in Drosophila.
- Drosophila developmental dynamics are non-Markov or Markov.
- The tested geometric/kinematic S is a complete current state.
- A positive `Delta R2` inside an inadequate cross-sequence model is evidence of state incompleteness.
- Two CTC acquisitions provide broad biological replication.
- These gold labels provide molecular state, force, polarity, chromatin, signaling, or complete morphology.

## Important limitations
- Only two acquisition sequences exist for the group-level cross-sequence test.
- The tracked cells are a challenge-defined developing nervous-system subset, not all embryonic cells.
- The present representation contains centroid geometry, label volume and recent kinematics only.
- Current-position centering/scaling is computed from frame-25 geometry within each sequence; this is current-state-only but transductive at the held-out group level.
- The secondary invariant analysis was introduced after the raw-vector domain-shift failure and is explicitly exploratory/sensitivity analysis.
- No permutation/calibration campaign is necessary for promotion because the analysis already fails the earlier absolute-adequacy gate.
