# T9 Claim Boundaries

## Allowed
- For fixed predictions/baseline, the exact MSE advantage changes as `G(a)=G(0)+2a<z,p-b>`; the quadratic injection term cancels.
- If the injection is orthogonal to `p-b`, fixed-predictor baseline-relative adequacy is invariant to amplitude.
- With centered injection, the corresponding constant mean baseline need not move.
- For centered test-mean R2, exact orthogonality preserves the fixed-predictor margin sign wherever target variance remains positive; nonzero alignment gives only one-sided monotonicity, not general sign invariance.
- Changes under R10–R12 can therefore be decomposed into alignment, baseline movement, and predictor refit/generalization effects.

## Not allowed
- Do not claim R10–R12 predictors are fixed; they are refit.
- Do not say T9 proves why R12 achieved 30/30 adequacy.
- Do not infer screening-off, memory, Markovity, or biological effect sizes.
- Do not claim mathematical novelty or priority.
