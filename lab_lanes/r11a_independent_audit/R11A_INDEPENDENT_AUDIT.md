The central numerical R11 sensitivity surface and the four-way R10 decomposition are reproducible from the committed artifacts, and the 0.30-SD arm is inherited rather than refit. The tested 0.15/0.30/0.45/0.60 grid supports the task-specific statement that aggregate Gate-2 detection increases while S-only adequacy decreases, with joint success equal to 0/20, 6/20, 6/20, and 6/20 respectively. Therefore larger injection alone does not rescue the strict joint criterion on this tested grid.

Two qualifications are required. First, the R10 preregistration states synthetic residual-direction seeds `20260830+r`, while the committed R10 implementation actually uses `20260830+500000+r`. R11 correctly reuses the implemented R10 seed family, so cross-scale pairing is preserved, but the phrase “exact R10 ... seeds” is only true for the executed pipeline, not for the literal preregistered seed text. Second, aggregate detection is monotone on the four tested grid points, but detection is not monotone replicate-by-replicate: replicate 4 passes Gate 2 at 0.45 and fails again at 0.60. The 30% joint plateau also consists of changing replicate identities. This is an empirical task/model/grid result, not a universal nonmonotonicity theorem.

### 4. Idealized R2 formula — NEEDS QUALIFICATION
R11 writes the idealized formula

`R2_S(a) = Var(m(S)) / [Var(m(S)) + Var(e) + a^2 Var(Z)]`.

For this exact expression, sufficient assumptions include:

1. `Y = m(S) + e` with `E[e|S]=0`, so `m(S)=E[Y|S]` and `Cov(m(S),e)=0`.
2. `E[Z|S]=0`, so the oracle predictor of `Y+aZ` from S remains `m(S)` and `Cov(m(S),Z)=0`.
3. `Cov(e,Z)=0` for the variance denominator and MSE cross term to omit `2a Cov(e,Z)`. Independence of Z and e is sufficient but stronger than necessary; zero covariance is enough for the displayed variance/MSE formula.
4. Finite second moments and nonzero outcome variance.
5. R2 is the population oracle coefficient of determination using squared-error prediction from S, not a finite-sample RF/ET test-fold R2.

Under these conditions, the oracle S-only MSE is `Var(e)+a^2 Var(Z)` and the displayed R2 decreases with `|a|` whenever `Var(Z)>0`. The actual R10/R11 construction only residualizes Z linearly against pooled S and therefore does not establish `E[Z|S]=0`, independence from e, or the exact oracle formula. The theory note correctly labels the expression idealized, but “independence assumptions” should be sharpened to distinguish conditional-mean orthogonality from the weaker zero-covariance condition needed for the cross term.

### 5. Larger injection alone cannot rescue the strict joint criterion on tested grid — PASS, task-specific only
The recomputed joint counts are 0/20, 6/20, 6/20, and 6/20. None approaches the R10 preregistered 80% requirement. Therefore, on the tested first-20 paired directions, frozen RF/ExtraTrees stack, two reciprocal acquisition folds, current adequacy definition, and scales 0.15–0.60, increasing the synthetic injection alone does not rescue the strict joint criterion.

This does **not** prove that larger amplitudes outside the grid cannot change joint success, nor that such a plateau is universal. It also does not prove a general nonmonotonicity theorem for joint calibration power.

## Leakage / aggregation / gate-change audit

### No outcome leakage found in registration or residual-direction construction
R10/R11 construct S and H from committed present/history columns and create the injected history direction without using Y in the Ridge residualizer. Target acquisition percentile registration is transductive and uses unlabeled target covariates; that is part of the frozen R9/R10 task and must not be described as target-free deployment.

### Important design dependence, not hidden leakage
The synthetic injection magnitude uses pooled `SD(Y)` across both acquisitions, and the residualizer `Ridge(S -> z)` is fit on the pooled two-acquisition S/H calibration table. These operations use no outcome labels for residualization, but the scale uses outcome dispersion from the complete calibration dataset. This is acceptable as the frozen planning stress test as written, but it means the calibration is not a strictly train-only simulation of deployment. Its interpretation should remain methodological sensitivity analysis.

### Aggregation weakness in code, but committed outputs are clean
The R11 aggregator concatenates matching chunk files, sorts by replicate, truncates to the first 20, and only checks `len >= 20`. It does not explicitly reject duplicate IDs or verify that the exact set is 0–19. A duplicated early replicate could theoretically displace a missing later replicate without raising an error. The committed artifacts do not have that defect: independent audit found exact 0–19 coverage with no duplicates for all newly fitted scales.

Recommended correction: aggregate by replicate ID and assert `sorted(ids)==list(range(20))` and uniqueness before summary generation.

### No post-hoc Gate-2 rule change found
R11 imports and calls R10 `fit_pair` and `gate2` directly. The committed R11 preregistration freezes the task, models, folds, history coordinates, registration, Gate-2 rule, and first 20 directions before the new-scale evaluations. No alternate gate appears in the committed R11 code or summary.

## Exact corrections recommended

1. Replace “same ... seeds” with: **“same implemented R10 residual-direction seed family (`SEED+500000+rep`); note that this differs from the literal `20260830+r` formula written in the R10 preregistration.”**
2. Replace broad wording such as “larger effects are easier to detect” with: **“Across the four tested paired scales, aggregate Gate-2 detection increased from 0/20 to 15/20 while aggregate S-only adequacy decreased from 17/20 to 9/20; individual directions need not be monotone.”**
3. Describe the 30% result as an **aggregate plateau on the tested grid** and note that its successful replicate identities change across 0.30, 0.45, and 0.60.
4. In the theory note, state that independence of `Z` and `e` is sufficient but not necessary; `Cov(e,Z)=0` suffices for the displayed cross-term-free variance formula, together with `E[Z|S]=0` and `E[e|S]=0`.
5. Harden the aggregator to reject duplicate/missing replicate IDs before truncation or summary.

## Safe methodological statement
A defensible summary is:

> In this two-acquisition Tribolium radial-velocity calibration, using the frozen RF/ExtraTrees pipeline and the same 20 implemented residual-history directions, increasing synthetic history injection from 0.15 to 0.60 target-SD increased aggregate Gate-2 detection while decreasing S-only absolute adequacy. The strict joint success rate was 0/20 at 0.15 and 6/20 at each of 0.30, 0.45, and 0.60, far below the preregistered 80% criterion. This is a task-, model-, sample-, and grid-specific planning result; it neither establishes a universal nonmonotonicity law nor resolves the biological history question.
