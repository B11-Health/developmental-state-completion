## Likely cross structure (planning, not an assertion of feasibility)

Smallest intended endpoint genotypes are RCOg-V / cuc2-4;RCOg-V / cyp71-3;RCOg-V (or nop2a-5 fallback), each carrying functional PIN1p::PIN1:GFP and the Hu inducible ChCUC1 system. Because selectable markers/linkage are not reported sufficiently in the audited papers, no generation count or marker-compatible crossing recipe is claimed. A two-step introgression (reporter then inducible system, or vice versa) is a reasonable planning model, but the holder lab must choose the actual crossing order after marker/linkage review.

## Optical feasibility gate

Wang reports sequential GFP 488/493-510 and VENUS 514/520-550 imaging with dye separation. That demonstrates that the group has handled GFP/VENUS together, but B2 still requires single-color and no-fluorophore controls in the **new triple-reporter combined lines**. ChCUC1:tdTomato adds a 561/575-600 channel. GO requires stable unmixing/quantification without genotype-dependent bleed-through. If not, retain RCOg-V genetically but omit RCO-VENUS as a quantitative simultaneous endpoint.
