## Facts rechecked in Hu / SI
- Inducible material: ChCUC1p::LhG4:GR; Op::ChCUC1:tdTomato transformed into Col-0 PIN1p::PIN1:GFP; 20 T2 lines isolated, two representative lines selected.
- Reference induction: dex stock 50 mM in DMSO; 10 uM dex + 0.01% Triton X-100 working solution; mock substitutes DMSO; spray when leaf 3 visible; 24 h later expose/image leaf 4; closely matched leaf lengths.
- Acute endpoint: PIN1 polarity / reversal-distance response at 24 h.
- Imaging: GFP 488/493-510; Venus 514/520-550; tdTomato 561/575-600.
- Published local growth/anisotropy measurements belong to a separate heat-shock mosaic system and are not dex physiological-cost validation in the proposed RCO crosses.
