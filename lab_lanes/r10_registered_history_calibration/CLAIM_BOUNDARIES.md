# R10 Claim Boundaries

## Allowed
- The R9-registered Tribolium radial present representation remains absolutely adequate in the observed R10 history comparison.
- Observed older-history Delta R2 is near zero and unstable in sign: mean -0.0030 RF and -0.0054 Extra Trees.
- The matched 100-permutation no-increment control has 0 Gate-2 passes.
- +0.30 target-SD known-incomplete calibration succeeds in only 10/30 replicates under the joint adequacy+Gate2 requirement, below the preregistered 80% threshold.
- Therefore the history question is calibration-limited/unresolved.

## Not allowed
- Do not claim screening-off, Markovity, memorylessness or that history is useless.
- Do not convert 0/100 null Gate-2 passes into a biological independence claim.
- Do not call +0.30 SD a measured biological effect size; it is synthetic calibration.
- Do not ignore the calibration failure because observed Delta R2 is small.
- Do not generalize beyond the one qualified Tribolium radial task and transductive deployment assumption.

## Program lesson
Absolute adequacy is necessary but not sufficient for a defensible history-screening conclusion. The sequence is now empirically demonstrated twice: first establish transfer adequacy, then demonstrate sensitivity of the history-increment test before interpreting a near-zero increment.
