# T8 Claim Boundaries

## Allowed
- In the audited simple oracle model, with `(S,H)` revealing `Z` and `E[E|S,H]=0`, joint thresholds are compatible iff `r0>=rho/(1-delta)`.
- The maximum simple-model incremental R2 compatible with `R2_S>=rho` is `1-rho/r0` when `r0>=rho`.
- If `(S,H)` also predicts base residual structure, the generalized augmented curve contains `q=Var(E[E|S,H])/B`.
- The envelope is a useful prospective calibration-design constraint.

## Not allowed
- Do not omit `E[E|S,H]=0` when stating the simple iff theorem.
- Do not claim exact revelation of `Z` alone is sufficient; T8A supplied a counterexample.
- Do not claim mathematical novelty or priority.
- Do not claim this exactly describes R10/R11/R12 finite-sample tree models or their RMSE gate; with only `rho=0`, the simple oracle R2 does not cross zero at finite injection and therefore does not explain empirical negative-R2 collapse.
- Do not infer biological memory, screening-off, Markovity, or a biological effect-size threshold.
- Do not claim any empirical 30%/53%/60% calibration rate is mathematically forced by T8.
