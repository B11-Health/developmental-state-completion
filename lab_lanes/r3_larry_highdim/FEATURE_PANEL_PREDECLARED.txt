# R3 predeclared day-2 expression panel

Frozen before any R3 outcome-model fit. The panel is a fixed 64-gene hematopoietic/state set chosen without consulting well-2 target labels or model performance. The checked-in R3 analysis uses the first 32 genes; the remaining genes are reserved and were not analyzed in this checkpoint. Expression is queried from the authors' release-native normalized-expression backend; scaling and dimensionality reduction are fit inside training folds.
