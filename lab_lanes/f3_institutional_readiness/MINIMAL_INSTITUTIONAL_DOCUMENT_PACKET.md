# Minimal Institutional Document Packet for NSF 26-520

This packet is the minimum evidence set F3 recommends assembling before any AOR certification. It intentionally excludes passwords, MFA secrets, EIN/UEI/CAGE values from the Git repository.

## Entity and authority

1. **Legal-entity evidence** — current organizational legal name and governing address from authoritative corporate records.
2. **Nonprofit/tax-status evidence** — IRS determination letter or other authoritative documentary evidence supporting any nonprofit/tax-exempt representation actually made.
3. **SAM entity-registration summary** — current active status, renewal date, legal-name/address match, and UEI retained in a secure administrative record rather than this public repo.
4. **Authority memo / board or officer record** — identifies who may administer federal grant systems and who is legally authorized to act as AOR; do not infer from titles alone.
5. **NSF organization record evidence** — screenshot/export or administrative note confirming the Research.gov organization record and UEI linkage after authenticated verification.

## Roles and people

6. **Institution Administrator record** — person, NSF ID, organization affiliation, and role status.
7. **PI affiliation/role record** — intended PI’s NSF ID, Code Gym affiliation, and organization-approved PI role.
8. **AOR record** — person, documented organizational authority, NSF ID, and approved AOR role.
9. **Sponsored-project routing note** — identifies SPO/pre-award equivalent and internal review path, even if a small organization combines functions in one authorized person.
10. **Senior/key roster** — final list driving biosketch, Current & Pending/Other Support, COA, and certification requirements.

## Compliance and finance

11. **Current SAM representations/certifications review record** — dated review by the authorized SAM administrator.
12. **Written COI policy and implementation owner** — plus any NSF-required certification support.
13. **Indirect-cost/F&A documentation** — negotiated agreement, documented permissible election, or other authoritative institutional basis actually used; never invent a rate.
14. **Compensation/fringe support** — payroll/board-approved compensation basis and fringe treatment sufficient to defend the budget.
15. **Financial controls/contact sheet** — payment/finance contact, record-retention owner, and basic grant-accounting responsibility.

## Proposal-specific institutional packet

16. **Budget and budget justification** with only documented rates and allocable costs.
17. **Facilities, Equipment and Other Resources** based on resources actually available.
18. **Data Management and Sharing Plan** aligned with real repositories/data stewardship.
19. **Subaward/collaboration documentation** only for real partners: scope, budget/justification, commitment, UEI/NSF registration as required by current rules.
20. **Current senior/key common forms** completed/certified through the current NSF process.
21. **Final compliance checklist** against the PAPPG in effect on the actual submission date and NSF 26-520.

## Secure-handling rule

Keep UEI, EIN, CAGE, account IDs, signed authority records, and tax documents in the organization’s controlled administrative storage. The Git repository should record only status, dates, non-sensitive evidence pointers, and whether each gate is green/red.
