## Critical-path dependencies

1. **Account/organization dependency:** PI/AOR/SPO verification cannot be closed until an authorized user can access the authenticated organization state.
2. **Authority dependency:** system role visibility does not substitute for actual institutional authority; AOR authority must be documented.
3. **Budget dependency:** F&A treatment, salary/fringe, and subaward costs cannot be finalized from guesses.
4. **Certification dependency:** COI, SAM reps/certs, and senior/key disclosures must be current before AOR submission.
5. **Science/admin coupling:** do not promise living experiments, collaborators, facilities, materials, or subawards unless the corresponding commitment exists.

## Slip rule

If organization/AOR readiness is not green by **2026-09-10**, immediately prepare the scientific package so it can be submitted later without damage, and evaluate a real partner-led structure only if a partner independently agrees. Do not invent a fiscal sponsor, proxy AOR, or institutional relationship. Because 26-520 accepts proposals anytime, correctness outranks the October target.
