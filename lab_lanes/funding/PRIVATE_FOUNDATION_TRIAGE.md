# F1 — Private-foundation LOI triage

Date: 2026-08-29

## Simons Foundation — Targeted Grants in MPS
**Ranking: CONDITIONAL / partner route.**

Official RFA text retrieved on 2026-08-29 states that the program supports high-risk theoretical mathematics, physics and computer science projects of exceptional promise; LOIs are rolling; funding can run up to five years with flexible level/duration; PIs/co-Is must hold tenured, tenure-track **or equivalent** positions at eligible institutions/centers; applications may be submitted by established U.S. or foreign public/private educational institutions and stand-alone research centers; for-profit institutions are ineligible.

**Scientific fit:** high for the theorem/counterexample/experimental-design mathematics if framed as genuinely high-risk foundational math/CS, not primarily as a wet-lab grant.

**Eligibility blocker:** the current audit does not verify that Code Gym is an “established ... stand-alone research center” under Simons' meaning, nor that the intended PI holds an “equivalent” position. Do not interpret nonprofit status alone as satisfying either condition.

**Decision:** merit an LOI only after one of two gates is met: (A) Simons eligibility of Code Gym + PI-equivalent status is documented; or (B) a clearly eligible academic/research-center PI/institution leads. Until then, do not submit.

## Alfred P. Sloan Foundation — Matter-to-Life
**Ranking: CONDITIONAL / concept-development only.**

The existing funding audit records the official Matter-to-Life program as supporting work on physical principles/mechanisms that distinguish living systems, including information processing, organization and functional capabilities, with a short LOI route and no annual deadline shown on the program page at the time of audit. The same audit explicitly left institutional eligibility unverified. A fresh direct fetch of the Sloan page from the shell was blocked by the site, so this lane does **not** upgrade any eligibility claim beyond that prior browser-verified record.

**Scientific fit:** moderate only if the project is reformulated around a real physical-principles question—e.g., constraints on information-bearing state variables and interventionally distinguishable organization in living systems. Merely relabeling developmental prediction as “matter-to-life” would be weak.

**Decision:** do not send an LOI yet. First produce a distinct <=2-page Matter-to-Life hypothesis that would still be compelling if all Arabidopsis-specific details were removed, and independently verify applicant/institution eligibility from Sloan before transmission.
