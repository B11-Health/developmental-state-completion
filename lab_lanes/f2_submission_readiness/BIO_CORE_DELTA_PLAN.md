# NSF 26-517 BIO Core / Developmental Systems - Delta Plan

Date: 2026-08-29
Status: **CONDITIONAL planning only**.

## Core rule
BIO Core cannot be a renamed MathBio proposal. Lead with a biological mechanism/question that can be tested in living material. Mathematics/statistics are supporting tools.

## Biological question that survives current audit
Can biologically distinct developmental backgrounds that are difficult to distinguish from a prespecified baseline be prospectively separated by a controlled response measurement, and does that response improve prediction of a declared future developmental endpoint without being explained by perturbation burden/stress?

This is task-, tissue-, stage-, measurement-, and intervention-specific. Do not claim a globally Markovian organism or a universal completed developmental state.

## Currently usable
1. Published RCOg-V suppressor system as a candidate ambiguity set.
2. Published inducible CUC1/PIN1 response biology as a candidate response interface; compatibility with exact RCOg-V suppressor backgrounds is not established.
3. Audited prospective design logic: baseline-only vs identical baseline + active-response features; blinded background key; frozen decoder/features; pilot-derived confirmatory N; burden controls; independent biological replication where feasible.
4. Public-data computational foundation as rationale/method development, not a substitute for living validation in a BIO-led proposal.

## Not yet executable - do not represent as available
- exact seed/material availability for all desired RCOg-V suppressors;
- stock-center identifiers where not independently verified;
- MTA/direct-transfer path;
- exact compatible crosses with inducible CUC1/PIN1 reporter backgrounds;
- segregation/time burden;
- validated dexamethasone dose/timing in combined backgrounds;
- validated mock/equal-cost burden-matched comparator;
- committed growth/chamber capacity;
- committed live/confocal imaging capacity;
- pilot variance and acquisition-failure rate;
- confirmatory sample size;
- prespecified physiological-burden threshold;
- independent coding/unblinding role;
- final applicable biosafety/GM-plant/material-transfer documentation.

If unresolved, describe as Phase-0 feasibility or remove from the confirmatory aim.

## BIO-specific aims after execution gates clear
### Aim 1 - Establish a feasible response assay
Verify material identity/transfer, build or obtain only compatible combinations, establish staging/imaging/induction window, quantify mock/induction burden and acquisition failure, and estimate variance for simulation-based confirmatory N.

Go criterion: reproducible biologically interpretable response without unacceptable burden and with feasible throughput.

### Aim 2 - Blinded prospective background/mechanism prediction
Compare baseline model (complete prespecified baseline stack) to active model (same baseline + preregistered intervention-response features). Freeze features, decoder, grouping/splits, intervention rule and prediction files before unblinding.

Success: stable held-out improvement on predefined hidden-background and future-development tasks.

Failure: no improvement; response dominated by stress/burden; baseline already resolves ambiguity; or effect fails independent replication.

### Aim 3 - Task-specific residual-history test
Ask whether older measured trajectory H adds stable held-out predictive value after current measured response state S for predefined future Y. Calibrate on matched known-complete and known-history-dependent generators. Non-significance alone is not evidence of completion.

## Required controls
- baseline-only comparator receives every baseline variable available to active model;
- vehicle/mock control;
- collaborator-selected physiological-burden readouts;
- stage/tissue/layer definitions frozen before confirmatory analysis;
- genotype/background blinded during feature extraction/model fitting;
- random/equal-cost perturbation comparator only if biologically valid and burden-matched;
- independent allele/background replication where scientifically meaningful;
- do not use a `pin1` perturbation if the critical readout requires normal PIN1 behavior unless an explicit alternative interpretation/readout is designed;
- external atlas annotations are not direct longitudinal specimen measurements;
- Phase-0 specimens are not confirmatory evidence.

## Collaborator/subaward gate
Before BIO submission, document:
1. Which exact lines are physically available?
2. Which are stock-center accessible vs direct-transfer only?
3. Exact genetic backgrounds?
4. Which crosses are feasible and what generation/time burden follows?
5. Which reporters/induction constructs can coexist without invalidating the readout?
6. What growth/staging/imaging capacity is committed?
7. Who performs genotyping, plant growth, microscopy, coding and unblinding?
8. What pilot sets dose, timing, burden limits, variance and failure rate?
9. What institutional biosafety/GM-plant/MTA requirements apply?
10. Will the collaborator be subawardee, separate collaborative proposer, or unfunded collaborator?

Until these answers exist, BIO Core remains **CONDITIONAL**.

## BIO narrative delta from MathBio
1. biological developmental mechanism/problem;
2. why baseline phenotype/state is insufficient for this exact biological question;
3. published candidate systems and what is actually known;
4. Phase-0 feasibility/material plan;
5. prospective blinded experiment;
6. mechanistic interpretation and alternatives;
7. quantitative state-sufficiency analysis as a tool, not headline;
8. replication/generalization;
9. data/reproducibility;
10. broader impacts.

## BIO no-go checklist
Do not submit if any remains true:
- [ ] no experimental collaborator with committed capacity;
- [ ] material/transfer path speculative;
- [ ] key crosses/construct compatibility unknown but presented as routine;
- [ ] no burden/stress-control strategy;
- [ ] no pilot-derived variance path for confirmatory N;
- [ ] no blinded prospective workflow;
- [ ] biology depends on a measurement invalidated by the perturbation itself;
- [ ] Research.gov/AOR/SPO/F&A/documentary gates unresolved;
- [ ] primary evidence remains released-data reanalysis/simulation rather than an executable biological experiment.
