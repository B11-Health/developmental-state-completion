# R4 Claim Boundaries

## Inherited boundaries

R4 preserves the durable evidence hierarchy already frozen by R1/R2/R3/M2/M3/B2/F3. R1 supports only a finite-sample, late-L1 FM1 task-specific screening-off result; it does not establish an intrinsic biological Markov state. R2 remains calibration-limited/unresolved despite observed nonlinear history gains. R3 is a higher-dimensional LARRY positive control using 32 measured genes, not a full-transcriptome proof. M2/M3 are design/theorem/simulation work and do not turn synthetic optimization guarantees into living biology. B2 remains a prospective living-study planning lane with confirmatory execution NO-GO at that checkpoint. F3 concerns institutional submission readiness and contributes no biological evidence.

## New R4 executable result: what it says

The GSE167135 Smart-seq2/FACS proxy analysis contains 621 released cells and 32,834 expression features after metadata-row removal. In matched-pool-index held-out prediction of TMMp versus ATML1p reporter enrichment, adding seven FACS measurements to train-only transcriptome PCs improved mean held-out AUC by +0.0620 (logistic), +0.0505 (random forest), and +0.0623 (histogram gradient boosting). Mean log-loss improvement was +0.1684, +0.0860, and +0.1749 respectively. The 20-replicate group-preserving logistic H-permutation null had mean AUC gain -0.0029 and maximum +0.0029. Known-complete calibration produced mean gain -0.0009; deliberately injected residual-H incompleteness produced mean +0.0566 (range +0.0190 to +0.1378).

**Allowed:** under this exact reporter-class proxy task and split, the selected 20-PC transcriptomic representation does not screen off same-time FACS information; the result is estimator-consistent and far larger than the checked permutation null.

**Not allowed:** “Arabidopsis developmental state is incomplete”; “the plant remembers its past”; “FACS reveals hidden causal memory”; “the transcriptome is insufficient for future development”; “TMMp is the future”; or any claim of exact conditional dependence/independence. H here is same-time FACS measurement and Y is same-time reporter enrichment, not history and future.

## Why this negative-for-completion result matters

The point of R4 is adversarial cross-system expansion, not confirmation. A pipeline that only promoted datasets where extra variables failed to help would be biased toward completion. Here, extra present measurements materially help a transcriptomic state for a technically clean proxy target. This demonstrates that representation choice can manufacture apparent screening-off or incompleteness even within one developmental dataset. It raises, rather than relaxes, the burden for future state-completion claims.

## Cross-sectional atlas boundary

For GSE106587, E-MTAB-6967, GSE112294, GSE119945, GSE113074 and similar destructive scRNA time courses, earlier and later cells are different cells. Pseudotime, RNA velocity, optimal transport, fate maps and trajectory trees are reconstructions. They can support useful prediction tasks but do not transform cross-sectional observations into literal cell histories/futures. R4 therefore labels them “trajectory/future proxies” unless a release-native lineage link or repeated same-unit measurement is verified.

## Intervention boundary

Treatment history can be H, but a prediction gain under treatment does not by itself show a causal memory variable. It may reflect dose, toxicity, developmental delay, batch, altered composition, or a present variable omitted from S. Intervention-indexed state sufficiency requires conditioning on a declared intervention family and adequate overlap/replication; it is stronger than pooled observational screening-off.

## Statistical boundary

Finite-sample non-detection is not exact conditional independence. A positive incremental score is also not mechanism. Calibration defines what magnitude this pipeline can detect on the observed geometry. If calibration power is inadequate, the correct disposition is unresolved, not a post-hoc rescue. Nulls and permutations diagnose some leakage/circularity modes but cannot certify absence of all confounding.

## Provenance boundary

Only genuinely public release artifacts were used. No unreleased data, private collaborator material, account creation, researcher contact, email, publication, or external submission occurred. Repository availability does not imply that Code Gym owns or may relicense those datasets.
